Privacy notice

Last updated 25 October 2026

In short: OpenJio is a pre-launch student project. If you sign up through our Facebook or Instagram form, we use your details only to keep you updated and, if you agree, to invite you to a short call. We don't sell or share them, and we delete them by 31 December 2026.

Who we are

OpenJio is a team of NUS students building an admissions planner for Singapore families as part of a university module. OpenJio is not yet a registered company and is not live.

What we collect

  • Your email address, as filled in on the sign-up form
  • Your answers to the form's questions, such as your child's school level, which plan you'd consider, or your interest in being a junior consultant
  • Whether you're happy to be contacted for a short call

We don't ask for your child's name, school, records or certificates, and we don't collect payment details.

How we use it

  • To tell you when OpenJio opens and what early access includes
  • To invite you to a short call about the idea, only if you said yes
  • To count interest in total (for example, how many people chose each plan) for our module report. Reports never name or identify anyone.

Who can see it

Only the OpenJio team. We don't sell, rent or share your details with tuition centres, schools, advertisers or anyone else. Meta processes the form under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We delete everything by 31 December 2026, unless you ask us to remove it sooner.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time, or unsubscribe from any email we send. Email us at joshuaho04@u.nus.edu and we'll act on it within 7 days.

We handle personal data in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).
