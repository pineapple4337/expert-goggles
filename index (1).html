DSA information is easy to find online. Keeping track of your own child is the hard part.

OpenJio keeps it all in one place, so you spend less time hunting and more time deciding.

  • 💬

    The robotics certificate is somewhere in the family WhatsApp chat, two years up.

  • 📅

  • 🧩

  • 🛤️

    Every stage has its own route. DSA-Sec, then DSA-JC or Poly EAE, then university, each with different rules.

How it works

Three things, done a little at a time, from P4 all the way to university applications.

  1. 1

    Collect achievements

    Snap a photo of a certificate and OpenJio reads the details for you, with quick feedback on what's missing. No certificate? It goes on your list.

  2. 2

    Explore options

    See schools or courses that match what your child already does, why each one appears, and what you still need to check. On Pro and Elite, the scan also learns from records other families choose to share.

  3. 3

    Follow the plan

    Tick off tasks, get reminders before each window and, on a paid plan, talk it through with a consultant every month or two.

                Questions you might have

                Can OpenJio get my child a place?

                No. DSA and other admissions decisions belong to schools and institutions. OpenJio helps you stay organised and plan. Nobody can promise an offer.

                Is it only for DSA-Sec?

                No. We start with DSA-Sec because the deadlines are fixed and it's the first big step. The same record carries on to DSA-JC, Poly EAE, university and scholarship applications.

                Who are the consultants?

                Junior consultants are university students who went through DSA themselves. Senior consultants are former coaches and educators. Pro includes a junior check-in every two months. Elite includes a senior check-in every month.

                What happens to the photos I upload?

                Certificates and other proof stay private to your family. On the free plan you get quick feedback on each upload. On Pro and Elite, uploads are read and filed automatically. Proof is never shared in the notes library.

                How does the school-fit scan use other families' records?

                Only records that families have agreed to share are used, with names and personal details removed. Until enough families take part, suggestions are based on your own record and each school's published information.

                How are tuition centres chosen for the list?

                They come from public information about each centre's subjects and branch areas. The list is not a ranking. When partner centres join, they will carry a "Partner" label because they pay us a referral fee, and free help at school will still show first.

                What happens to what I type in the sample planner?

                Nothing leaves your browser. The sample planner keeps changes in this tab only, and they disappear when you close or reload the page.
